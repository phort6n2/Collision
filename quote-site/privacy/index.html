Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026 · Applies to collisionglass.co

This policy describes what Collision Auto Glass & Calibration ("we", "us") collects through this website, why we collect it, who we share it with, and what you can ask us to do about it. It is written specifically for this site rather than being a generic template, so it names the actual fields, the actual third parties, and the actual tracking involved.

1. Who we are

Collision Auto Glass & Calibration, 14201 NW Science Park Dr, Portland, OR 97229. Phone (503) 656-3500. Email glass@collisionautoglass.com. We are a family-owned auto glass and calibration business operating shops in Cedar Mill and Tualatin, Oregon. Oregon does not operate a state registration scheme for auto glass or motor vehicle repair businesses, so there is no registration number for us to publish — if you see one claimed by a shop in this state, it is worth asking what it actually is.

3. Advertising identifiers and analytics we capture automatically

Most visitors reach this site by clicking one of our ads. When that happens the advertising platform adds an identifier to the web address, and we read and keep it so we can tell which advertising actually produced an enquiry. Specifically, we capture whichever of the following are present:

  • Google click identifiers — gclid, gbraid, wbraid, gclsrc
  • Other platform click identifiers — msclkid (Microsoft), fbclid (Meta), ttclid (TikTok), li_fat_id (LinkedIn)
  • Campaign parameters — utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_term, utm_content
  • Page context — the page you landed on, the page you submitted from, and the referring website if your browser sent one

These are stored in your browser's session storage — not in a long-lived cookie — which means they persist while you browse this site and are discarded when you close the tab. If you submit the form, they are sent along with it so we can attribute the enquiry.

Google tag

We load Google's tag (gtag.js) to measure ad conversions and, where configured, site analytics. Google may set cookies in your browser to do this. Google's handling of that data is governed by its own privacy policy and how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Enhanced conversions — what actually gets sent

When you submit the form we use a Google Ads feature called enhanced conversions. Your email address and phone number are hashed in your browser before they are transmitted — Google's tag applies a one-way cryptographic hash locally, so the plain-text values are not sent to Google by this mechanism. The hashes let Google match the conversion to the ad click that produced it. We do not use this feature to send Google your name, address, vehicle or VIN.

4. Call tracking and dynamic phone numbers

Some phone numbers displayed on this site are tracking numbers that may change between visitors. This is how we tell which advertising generated a phone call. When you call a tracking number, our call platform records call metadata — the number you dialled, your caller ID, the date, time and duration of the call, and which advertising source your visit was attributed to — and connects you to us.

The number shown in the footer identity block is a real shop line and is deliberately not a tracking number. It is the number we give Google for its call asset, and it is excluded from the number-swapping described above so that it always reaches us directly.

Regardless of what Oregon law requires of us, our own commitment is simpler: if a call is being recorded you will be told at the start of it, and you may decline and speak to us on an unrecorded line instead.

A note on targeted advertising

Passing advertising identifiers and hashed contact details to Google for conversion measurement is processing for targeted advertising, even though no money changes hands and we do not regard it as a sale. Under the Oregon Consumer Privacy Act you have the right to opt out of processing for targeted advertising — see section 8, or contact us using the details there and we will action it.

6. Phone, text and email contact

By submitting the quote form you agree that we may contact you by phone, text message and email about your enquiry, your quote and your appointment. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text message to opt out of texts, or ask us on a call or by email and we will stop. Opting out of marketing does not stop us contacting you about a job already in progress.

7. How long we keep it

Attribution identifiers held in your browser are discarded when you close the tab. Enquiries that do not become jobs are retained in our CRM for up to 24 months so we can pick up the conversation if you come back. Records relating to work actually performed — including the invoice and warranty record — are kept for as long as our tax and accounting obligations require, and for as long as the lifetime workmanship guarantee on your vehicle could be claimed against. Because that guarantee runs for as long as you own the vehicle, the installation record is kept indefinitely; that is what allows us to honour a claim years later.

8. Your Oregon privacy rights

The Oregon Consumer Privacy Act came into effect on 1 July 2024. It applies to businesses that process the personal data of at least 100,000 Oregon residents, or of at least 25,000 residents where a quarter or more of revenue comes from selling personal data. A two-shop auto glass business is comfortably below both thresholds, so the OCPA very probably does not apply to us as a matter of law.

We would rather honour the rights than argue about the threshold. So if you are an Oregon resident, we will treat you as having the following rights, whether or not the statute compels us to:

  • Know what personal information we have collected about you, where it came from, why we collected it and who we disclosed it to
  • Access a copy of that information in a portable form
  • Correct inaccurate personal information
  • Delete personal information we hold about you, subject to narrow exceptions — we will keep the installation and warranty record for a vehicle we have worked on, because that is the document the lifetime guarantee is claimed against
  • Opt out of targeted advertising, of any sale of personal data, and of profiling used to make significant decisions — see the note in section 5
  • Obtain a list of the specific third parties to which we have disclosed personal data. This right is distinctive to Oregon, and section 5 of this page is our standing answer to it
  • Withhold consent for sensitive data — although we do not collect sensitive personal data through this site at all. Note that Oregon treats precise geolocation as sensitive and bans its sale outright; we neither collect it here nor sell it
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights. We will not refuse you service, change your price, or give you a lesser standard of service because you asked

To exercise any of these rights, email glass@collisionautoglass.com with the subject line "Oregon privacy request", or call (503) 656-3500. We will verify your identity by matching what you give us against what we hold — usually the phone number or email you used — and respond within the timeframes the statute requires. You may use an authorised agent; we will ask for proof of their authority.

You can also reduce ad tracking directly: Google offers My Ad Center and an Analytics opt-out browser add-on, and most browsers let you block or clear cookies and site storage.

9. Children

This site is for adults arranging vehicle glass work. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

This site is served over HTTPS and form submissions are encrypted in transit. Our CRM is access-controlled. No system is perfectly secure, and we will not pretend otherwise — but we do not store payment card details on this website at all, and we ask you not to send us card numbers by email.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or who we share it with, we will update this page and the "last updated" date above.

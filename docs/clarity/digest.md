## What to do with this

See `docs/clarity-loop.md`. Short version: take the single worst row, open
Clarity, filter replays to that page **and** the matching `form_outcome` tag,
watch five sessions, and only then decide whether there is a change to make.

Do not chase a row that moved by less than a handful of sessions. At this
traffic volume that is noise, and acting on it is how a site gets worse slowly.
